# Fake Cookie Generator

Fake Cookie Generator creates realistic-looking fake Roblox .ROBLOSECURITY cookies for demonstrations, testing, and educational purposes. Every cookie is randomly generated and does not belong to a real Roblox account.

This tool can be useful for showing how cookie scams work, creating cybersecurity demonstrations, or sending harmless fake cookies to scammers who ask for your real one. None of the generated cookies can be used to log into Roblox or access anyone's account.
